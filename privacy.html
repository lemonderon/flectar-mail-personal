Privacy policy

Flectar Mail Personal · Updated 8 November 2026

Scope and operator

This policy covers the personal Google OAuth registration named Flectar Mail Personal, operated by GitHub user lemonderon. Contact the operator through the repository issue tracker. It is intended for the operator’s own accounts. It is not an official policy issued by the upstream Flectar developers.

Google information accessed and its purpose

When you authorize this registration in Flectar Mail, the installed application may access your Google account identity, including your email address and profile information, and your Gmail messages, attachments, labels, and related mailbox information. The full Gmail permission allows reading, composing, sending, organizing, modifying, and permanently deleting messages.

Google user data obtained through this registration is used only to provide the user-facing email functionality you authorize. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Use of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Processing and storage

The email application runs on your device and synchronizes with Google. Flectar documents local storage of synchronized mailbox data and use of the operating-system keyring for account credentials on Linux. This registration does not add a separately operated server for processing mail. Local mailbox storage is not represented here as application-level encrypted storage; protect your device and backups.

This static website does not receive or store Google OAuth tokens or mailbox contents. Do not post personal information, passwords, tokens, or message contents in public support requests.

Sharing and optional integrations

Using email exchanges data with Google and, when you send messages, the recipients and their mail providers. Features or integrations you explicitly enable in Flectar may contact additional services; review their settings and policies before enabling them. Do not configure an integration to use Google user data for advertising, sale, or generalized AI-model training.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Mailbox data cached by the installed client can remain on your device until you remove it using the application’s account or storage controls or delete the relevant application data. You can revoke this registration’s Google access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase existing local copies, backups, or messages stored by Google or recipients. Google data is retained only as needed for the authorized mail functionality or until the user removes it.

Website hosting

The website contains no application-added analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, or Google login form. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and changes

Contact the operator through the repository issue tracker about this registration or its data practices. Do not post private information in public issues. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. Consult the upstream Flectar Mail documentation for the software’s behavior and available controls.
